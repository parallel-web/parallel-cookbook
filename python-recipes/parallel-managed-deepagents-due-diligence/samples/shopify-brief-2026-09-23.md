## 2. Financial snapshot

Shopify reported Q2 revenue of **$3,583 million**, up **34% year over year** from $2,680 million (33% constant-currency growth). Its revenue lines were **Merchant solutions $2,781 million** (+37%) and **Subscription solutions $802 million** (+22%). GMV was **$115,567 million**, +32%, and should not be conflated with revenue. [Shopify’s August 5 Q2 release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/05/3339164/0/en/shopify-delivers-big-30-growth-across-gmv-revenue-gross-profit-and-free-cash-flow.html)

**Profitability.** Gross profit was **$1,708 million**, +31% year over year; calculated gross margin was **47.7%** ($1,708 ÷ $3,583), versus **48.6%** a year earlier ($1,302 ÷ $2,680). GAAP operating income was **$488 million**, +68%; calculated operating margin was **13.6%**, versus 10.9%.

Revenue mix is a plausible contributor to the modest gross-margin decline, but that is **analysis, not management attribution**. The disclosed revenue and costs permit component calculations: Subscription solutions gross profit was $639 million ($802 less $163), or **79.7%** gross margin; Merchant solutions gross profit was $1,069 million ($2,781 less $1,712), or **38.4%**. Merchant solutions rose faster and represented 77.6% of revenue, versus 75.5% a year earlier. Thus, a greater mix of the lower-calculated-margin line can pressure consolidated gross margin. [Q2 release/financial statements](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/05/3339164/0/en/shopify-delivers-big-30-growth-across-gmv-revenue-gross-profit-and-free-cash-flow.html)

**Cash generation.** Net cash provided by operating activities was **$658 million** and free cash flow (FCF) was **$654 million**, versus $428 million and $422 million, respectively, in Q2 2025. Shopify defines this **non-GAAP** FCF as operating cash flow less capital expenditures; its reported FCF margin was **18%** ($654 ÷ $3,583), versus 16%. However, comparability deserves caution: starting in April 2026, Shopify presented merchant-cash-advance cash flows in **investing** rather than operating activities, consistent with loan treatment. The returned evidence does not establish whether the comparative-period presentation was recast, so the reported cash-flow increase should not be attributed solely to operating improvement. [Q2 release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/05/3339164/0/en/shopify-delivers-big-30-growth-across-gmv-revenue-gross-profit-and-free-cash-flow.html)

At June 30, cash and cash equivalents were **$1,656 million** and marketable securities were **$3,291 million**—separate balances totaling **$4,947 million**. [Q2 2026 Form 10-Q](https://shopifyinvestors.gcs-web.com/static-files/0378506c-ac85-467f-9675-980c6146784c)

**Net income requires qualification.** GAAP net income was **$1,502 million**, including a **$1,249 million unrealized gain on equity and other investments**. Shopify’s own non-GAAP reconciliation removes equity investments marked to market, net of tax, of **$1,063 million**, producing non-GAAP “net income excluding the impact of equity investments” of **$439 million**. The latter is not a substitute for GAAP net income, but it illustrates why the headline GAAP result is not a direct operating-profit measure. [10-Q](https://shopifyinvestors.gcs-web.com/static-files/0378506c-ac85-467f-9675-980c6146784c); [reconciliation](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/05/3339164/0/en/shopify-delivers-big-30-growth-across-gmv-revenue-gross-profit-and-free-cash-flow.html)

The expansion in operating margin alongside strong FCF margin is consistent with **operating leverage**—operating expenses growing more slowly than revenue—but FCF margin is a cash measure, not an operating-margin measure, and is affected by the presentation change above.

## Same-thread margin follow-up

In **Q2 2026**, Merchant solutions (“merchant services”) grew faster than Subscription solutions and became a larger share of revenue. Because its calculated gross margin was much lower, that mix shift likely pressured Shopify’s consolidated gross margin.

| Q2 2026, US$ millions | Subscription solutions | Merchant solutions | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | $802 | $2,781 | $3,583 |
| YoY revenue growth | 22% | 37% | 34% |
| Cost of revenue | $163 | $1,712 | $1,875 |
| Calculated gross profit | $639 | $1,069 | $1,708 |
| **Calculated gross margin** | **79.7%** | **38.4%** | **47.7%** |

*Calculations: gross profit = revenue less disclosed cost of revenue; gross margin = calculated gross profit ÷ revenue.* [Shopify’s Q2 2026 release, August 5, 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/05/3339164/0/en/shopify-delivers-big-30-growth-across-gmv-revenue-gross-profit-and-free-cash-flow.html)

### Gross-margin implication

- **Merchant solutions grew 37%,** versus **22%** for Subscription solutions.
- Merchant solutions consequently increased from **75.5% of revenue** in Q2 2025 ($2,024m ÷ $2,680m) to **77.6%** in Q2 2026 ($2,781m ÷ $3,583m).
- Subscription solutions, with the much higher calculated gross margin, fell from **24.5% to 22.4%** of revenue.

This mix shift is consistent with consolidated gross profit rising **31%**, slower than revenue’s **34%** growth, and consolidated calculated gross margin declining to **47.7%** from **48.6%** in Q2 2025. This is analytical inference from the disclosed line-item revenues and costs—not a management-stated causal attribution.

There was also a modest change in the component margins themselves: calculated Subscription solutions margin declined from **81.6%** in Q2 2025 ($535m gross profit on $656m revenue) to 79.7%, while Merchant solutions margin increased from **37.9%** ($767m on $2,024m) to 38.4%. The larger effect on the consolidated rate appears to be the greater revenue weighting of Merchant solutions.

### Operating and cash-flow margins differ

The revenue mix effect should not be conflated with operating leverage. Despite the lower gross margin, GAAP operating income rose **68%** to $488 million; calculated operating margin expanded to **13.6%** from 10.9%. That pattern is consistent with operating expenses growing more slowly than revenue, though it does not prove causation.

Shopify’s reported **free-cash-flow margin** improved to **18%** from 16%. However, Shopify changed the presentation of merchant-cash-advance cash flows starting in April 2026, moving them from operating to investing cash flows. Therefore, the reported operating-cash-flow/FCF comparison warrants caution and should not be attributed simply to revenue mix or operating performance. [Shopify Q2 release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/05/3339164/0/en/shopify-delivers-big-30-growth-across-gmv-revenue-gross-profit-and-free-cash-flow.html)
